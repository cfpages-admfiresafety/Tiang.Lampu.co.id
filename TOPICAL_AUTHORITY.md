# Topical Authority — tiang.lampu.co.id

## Role and boundary

`tiang.lampu.co.id` should become an Indonesian engineering-informed decision hub for lighting poles and closely related public-space poles: PJU/street-light poles, high-mast systems, floodlight and garden poles, solar-light poles, traffic-signal supports, plus adjacent CCTV, antenna, telecom, and lightning-protection supports where the pole/interface knowledge genuinely transfers.

The audited homepage calls the site “Spesialis Tiang Lampu Jalan #1” and promotes installation, energy-efficient lighting, PJU solar, LED lighting, garden/road/building applications, and other pole services. This is commercial positioning, not proof of market rank, structural-design authority, licensed electrical work, photometric competence, certified products, or completed projects. Neutral articles must never imply those qualifications without verifiable evidence.

Commercial quote, product, installation, availability, warranty, and contact intent stays on clear service routes. Engineering pages educate readers and prepare specifications; they do not supply a project-specific structural calculation, foundation design, photometric design, lightning-risk assessment, electrical design, traffic-management plan, lift plan, permit, or compliance declaration.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Structural/wind/foundation content requires current design basis, site inputs, drawings, and qualified structural/geotechnical review.
- Electrical, earthing, control, solar, surge, and lightning content requires current primary standards and licensed/competent electrical or lightning-protection review.
- Photometric claims require reproducible files/assumptions, maintained-light calculations, luminaire data, and a lighting professional.
- Traffic-signal/support content requires road authority and traffic engineer review; high-mast and lifting content requires manufacturer, lifting engineer, and K3 review.
- Never invent dimensions, wind speeds, anchor sizes, concrete grades, illuminance values, spacing, IP/IK ratings, cable sizes, torque, SWL, prices, standards clauses, permits, warranties, certificates, projects, or test results.
- Use original/licensed diagrams and field photos. Label conceptual details and calculators with inputs, assumptions, units, limits, and stop conditions.
